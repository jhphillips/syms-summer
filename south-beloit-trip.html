🏖️ Pearl Lake — Book a Cabana Ahead

Cabanas book up. Reserve online before you go. Monday will be noticeably less crowded than Sunday. Snack bar is on site so keep packing light.

🎆 Pops on the Rock — Get There Early

Free event, big crowd. Bring lawn chairs or blankets. Park early and walk in. Food trucks on site. Extended show this year for America's 250th — worth staying for the whole thing.

🎮 Coral Cove — Bring Socks

Socks are required for the bounce houses. Heads up especially for the kids. Open Sunday noon–8pm so you've got a solid window after Rachel's crew checks in.

🐕 Five Dogs

Nature at the Confluence and the Dorr Road trail are good dog-friendly options. Pearl Lake beach itself is not dog-friendly — plan those days accordingly.

🌊 Pearl Lake Water Clarity

It's an Illinois lake — the water's a bit murky, not crystal clear. That said, the facility itself has been majorly upgraded. Worth it, especially with a cabana.

🌧️ Rainy Day Backup

Coral Cove is fully indoors — solid option if it rains. Logan Museum of Anthropology in Beloit is another good one. Or just let the kids explore the two houses.

🎨 Creative Kit

MPC, Reason, iPad, sketchpad, two laptops. Don't force it — let whatever wants to come out, come out. Tuesday is probably your best window after Rachel's crew leaves.

Tilley's Pizza House

Beloit institution since 1955. Four generations, thin square-cut pizza, homemade sauce, sausage with fennel seeds. Locals are religious about this place. Dine in, carry out, or delivery.

📍 900 4th St, Beloit, WI · ~10 min drive

🥩 Nicer Night Out

🥩

Merrill & Houston's Steak Joint

Classic steakhouse inside the industrial-chic Ironworks Hotel. Brick walls, black-and-white history photos, live music on Fridays. Order the Ironworks — ribeye topped with shrimp, scallops, lobster and breadcrumbs. Good if you want a proper grown-up dinner.

📍 Downtown Beloit, WI · ~10 min drive

Velvet Buffalo

Modern Italian inside boutique Hotel Goodwin. Rotating seasonal menu, locally sourced ingredients, award-winning wine list. Casual-classy vibe. Chef sources produce at the Beloit Farmers Market. Best date night or grown-up dinner in the area.
